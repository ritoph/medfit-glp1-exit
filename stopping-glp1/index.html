Questions people ask about coming off GLP-1s

What happens when you stop a GLP-1 medication?

Many people find appetite and food noise come back, and studies show many regain some of the weight they lost within a year. A plan for food, habits and maintenance helps. That is what the GLP-1 Exit Program covers with Dr. Tran.

How do I come off weight loss medication without regaining weight?

No one can promise zero regain, but structure helps: enough protein, regular meals, strength training and support as appetite returns. Dr. Tran builds that plan with you over three months. Book a free consult to start.

Should I taper off my GLP-1 medication or stop all at once?

Talk with the clinician who prescribes it about your dose. This program does not prescribe, taper or adjust GLP-1 medication; it covers the nutrition, mindset and maintenance side of coming off.
