Questions people ask

How does the program help you keep weight off?

Many people find old habits return after a weight-loss program. A plan for food, strength, and maintenance helps. That is what Keep Your Progress covers with Dr. Tran.

Can the program promise I will never regain weight?

No one can promise zero regain. Structure helps: enough protein, regular meals, strength training, and coaching. Dr. Tran builds that plan with you over three months. Book a free consult to start.

How do you protect muscle?

The plan pairs a custom meal plan with enough protein and strength-focused coaching. Results vary by person.
